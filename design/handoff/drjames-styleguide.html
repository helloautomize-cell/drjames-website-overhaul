Style guide · v1.0 · October 2026

Dr. James Pallivathukkal — refined, not rebuilt.

The design system for drjames.vercel.app. It keeps the site's navy, paper, gold and teal identity, the serif headlines and the gold italic accent word. It fixes the issues found in the audit: contrast, a hero covered by the cookie banner, too much pinned scrolling, unclickable cards, weak photos and missing icons. Every component below is the target for Windsurf.

Build notes for Windsurf

  1. Refine the existing components. Do not rebuild the site. Keep routes, content, SEO metadata and JSON-LD. Change styling, layout and motion to match this guide.
  2. Tokens first. Copy the :root block into globals.css (or map it to tailwind.config theme.extend). Replace every hard-coded hex with a token. Do not add colours.
  3. Fonts. Check app/layout.tsx. If the display serif is not Newsreader, keep the existing serif and map it to --f-display. Load fonts with next/font, never a CSS @import.
  4. Small gold text (eyebrows, labels, under 18px) uses --gold-dark. --gold is for 24px+ display text and decoration only. Gold on navy uses --gold-2.
  5. Never fade text with opacity to show an inactive state. Use --ink-2 on paper and --muted-navy on navy.
  6. Scroll choreography is deliberate: the first-visit journey is the one long pinned section (one step per ~100vh, rail clickable). The conditions scene pins for ≤ 1 viewport or becomes a static grid with a stagger reveal. Everything else uses reveal-once motion. Use native sticky plus scroll position (or GSAP ScrollTrigger if it is already in the repo). Never hijack the scroll wheel.
  7. Every card that looks clickable is a link, with a hover state, focus ring and arrow.
  8. Images go through next/image with width/height, sizes, AVIF/WebP and a descriptive alt. Only the hero image gets priority. Images stay in their current paths until approved replacements arrive (see the image map from the crawl).
  9. Icons are PNG badges (1024², transparent, exported to 256 and 128) placed inside the CSS .badge shell (orbit, coin, sheen). Until the PNGs exist, use the inline SVG placeholders shown here.
  10. All motion is disabled under prefers-reduced-motion. Autoplay pauses on hover, focus and touch and has a visible pause button.
  11. Anything marked [CONFIRM] needs the doctor's sign-off before it ships.

Do

  • Accent exactly one word or phrase per headline in gold italic (whole picture, care, you).
  • Use teal for every primary action, and only for actions.
  • Alternate paper and navy sections to give the page rhythm. Never put two navy sections in a row.
  • Use the arch only for the logo and the badge coins. Photos never get frames: they bleed to the edge and dissolve into the surface (mask fade).

Don't

  • Use gold #8A6A35 for text under 18px.
  • Use red or coral for anything except emergencies (no red "sale" badges, no red errors in general UI).
  • Add new accent colours, gradients or a red cross symbol.
  • Fade text with opacity to show an inactive or secondary state.
  • Put photos inside borders, arches, rounded "window" frames or polaroid cards.

    Visual variety — beyond icons

    Icons are one tool, not the whole toolkit. A world-class medical site feels human and editorial, so every section uses a different device. Badges are reserved for three places: the conditions grid, the How he works principles and the first-visit journey. Everywhere else, use the devices below.

    Editorial photography

    Bleeds to the edge and fades into the surface. No frames. Used in the hero, How he works and the doctor profile.

    24years
    Big-number typography

    Serif numerals that count up once. Used for credentials, step numbers and career years.

    Normal <5.7%PrediabetesDiabetes ≥6.5%
    6.1
    Educational data visuals

    Range bars for HbA1c, BP, eGFR and the like on condition pages. They teach without alarming, and the patient can place their own number.

    James Pallivathukkal
    Human marks

    His real signature, handwritten-style notes and "in his own words" quotes. Trust comes from the person, not from symbols.

    Local texture

    A faint lattice taken from the hospital's own mashrabiya screens, used as section dividers and card backs. It is quietly Omani.

    Consulting today until 9 pm
    Live status

    Real opening hours (Asia/Muscat time) in the header, the doctor profile and the visit section. It makes the site feel alive.

    Maps & journeys

    The training map, the visit map and the first-visit path. Show a sequence as a path, not as a list.

    “the right understanding”
    Pull quotes

    A large serif quote with one gold word. Use at most one per page, so each one lands.

    Do

    • Pick one primary device per section and let it lead.
    • Prefer real content (photos, his words, real numbers) over decoration.
    • Use badges only for the conditions, principles and first-visit journey.

    Don't

    • Put an icon on every list item, row or heading.
    • Use two different icon styles on one page.
    • Use stock medical clip-art (red crosses, pill bottles, generic stethoscopes).

    Photography rules

    Photos are never framed. They bleed to the section edge and fade into it. Every photo of the doctor is enhanced, not replaced. Same face, same features, same build and age. No changes to skin tone, hairline or teeth beyond normal retouching. The doctor approves each image before it ships.

    PLACEHOLDER · ENHANCE
    Hero cut-outA clean transparent cut-out with no halo, even studio light, crisp coat and stethoscope. 1600px tall, PNG→WebP.
    PLACEHOLDER · ENHANCE
    In clinicSharper and warmer, with neutral whites. Recompose to 4:5 so the monitor doesn't cut the frame. Keep the real room.
    PLACEHOLDER · ENHANCE
    Hospital lobbyCrop so the background portrait is out of frame, or blur it heavily. Lift exposure and keep the green seating for colour harmony.

    Prompt pattern for ChatGPT

    • "Enhance this photo professionally. Keep the person's identity, face, features and expression exactly the same. Improve sharpness, lighting and colour to a warm, natural, premium editorial look. Neutral white balance. Do not change clothing details, age or body shape. Output 4:5, high resolution."

    Never

    • Generate a new face, or use a stock "doctor" as if it were Dr James.
    • Show stock patients as real patients, or before/after results.
    • Include the national portrait, hospital signage or other people's faces without permission.
    • Ship an image the doctor hasn't approved.

    Calm and purposeful. The current site over-choreographs scrolling; this budget replaces it.
