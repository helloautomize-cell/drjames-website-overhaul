# Home page redesign v2: colours, type, layout, header, hero and conditions

Read this whole prompt before you change anything. It replaces parts of DEVIN-MASTER-PROMPT.md. Where the two disagree, THIS prompt wins.
Branch: redesign/home-v2. Commit in the order of the sections below (one commit per section). Open one PR with a Vercel preview, post the screenshots listed under "Acceptance", then STOP. Do not touch sections of the home page that this prompt does not name, except to apply the new tokens.

## 0. What is removed from the current build
- Hero "credential cube" (master prompt 4.2) and the headline "Many conditions. One clear plan."
- "Find care by concern" pill row under the hero (4.3). The symptom finder in the header replaces it.
- "How Dr. James can help" tabs + ServiceGlassCards carousel on the home page (4.6). The new Conditions mosaic (section 7) replaces it. Keep the components for the /conditions/ hub if they are used there.
- Conditions marquee (4.9).
- All gradients on buttons, tabs and bars: --grad-brand, --grad-btn, --grad-bar. Petrol is a flat colour now.
- White page ground. The page ground is pearl; cards stay white.
- Sand tokens (#C9A46A and friends). Gold replaces them.
- The Omani arch as a photo frame and any rotating ring around the portrait. Do not frame the hero portrait in an arch or a circle.

## 1. Positioning and copy rules (all pages)
- Core identity: Specialist Internist. Never "Diabetes Specialist" as the identity, never "treats everything", never "longevity doctor".
- Promise: "A physician who sees the whole picture."
- Line under it: "Comprehensive internal medicine for long-term health."
- Four pillars, always in this order and wording: Diabetes and metabolic health · Complex adult medicine · Preventive care · Critical care experience.
  "Critical care experience" is a credential (he led ICU and COVID-19 operations), not a bookable service. Never link it to a booking reason.
- Specialties he does not hold (cardiology, neurology, rheumatology, haematology and so on) appear only as conditions he manages.
- No testimonials, ratings, patient numbers, "best", "leading", cure or guarantee claims, and no comparison with other doctors.
- Emergency line 9999 wherever symptoms are discussed. No em dashes or en dashes anywhere in copy.
