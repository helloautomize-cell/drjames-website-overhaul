GLP-1 medicines reduce appetite, increase fullness and slow stomach emptying, helping many people eat less and lose weight. Social media often shows dramatic before-and-after stories, but results vary widely. Understanding realistic expectations helps you make an informed decision.

For the wider picture, see our guide on [GLP-1 for Diabetes & Weight Loss: Results, Cost & Side Effects](/health-library/glp-1-diabetes-weight-loss-results-cost-side-effects/).

## What Does the Research Show?

Clinical trials combined medicines with diet and activity counselling. Average results were:

- Semaglutide 2.4 mg weekly: around 15 percent average weight loss in people without diabetes

- Tirzepatide at higher doses: around 20 percent average weight loss in people without diabetes

- Lower average weight loss in people with type 2 diabetes

Averages hide a wide range. A minority lost little weight, while others lost more than average.

## What Is a Realistic Timeline?

- First 1 to 3 months: doses are increased gradually, and weight loss starts slowly

- 3 to 6 months: steady loss as the dose reaches maintenance

- 6 to 18 months: weight loss continues more slowly, then usually plateaus

Weight loss is not linear, and plateaus are normal.

## What Affects How Much You Lose?

- The specific medicine and dose reached

- How well side effects are tolerated

- Diet quality and portion control

- Physical activity, especially strength training

- Sleep, stress and medical conditions

- Whether you have type 2 diabetes

- Medicines that promote weight gain

## Why Does Muscle Matter?

Some weight lost on GLP-1 medicines comes from muscle, not just fat. Eating enough protein and doing regular strength training help protect muscle, which supports metabolism and strength.

## What Other Benefits Are There?

Besides weight loss, people may see improvements in blood sugar, blood pressure and cholesterol. Some GLP-1 medicines have shown cardiovascular benefits in certain high-risk patients.

## What If You Lose Little Weight?

If weight loss is limited after several months at the target dose, your doctor may review the dose, check for other causes or discuss alternative treatments.

GLP-1 medicines can deliver meaningful weight loss, but they work best as part of a broader plan. Realistic expectations make long-term success more likely.
