## Who should see a physician about this

- You are over 40 and have not had a proper check in a few years.
- You want a baseline before a milestone: new job, marriage, insurance, travel.
- Family history of diabetes, heart disease, stroke or cancer worries you.
- You had an abnormal result elsewhere and want it interpreted.
- You smoke, carry extra weight, or sit most of the day and want a real assessment.

## What happens at your visit

The consultation comes first. Dr. James asks about your history, family history, diet, activity, sleep, smoking and alcohol, then examines you: pressure, weight, waist, heart and chest.

Only then are tests chosen. Each check-up is planned by Dr. James after a consultation, based on your conditions and symptoms, so no test is repeated or wasted. When results return, you sit down again and go through them in plain language: what is normal, what needs watching, and what needs action.

## How to prepare

- Ask when booking whether fasting is needed; sugar and some fat tests prefer eight to twelve hours fasting.
- Bring older reports so trends can be compared rather than single values judged alone.
- Bring your medicine and supplement list.

## After your visit

Results are explained one by one, not handed over as a confusing printout. You leave with the baseline recorded, any actions agreed, and a reminder interval for the next check. Anything abnormal becomes a proper plan, not a vague warning.
