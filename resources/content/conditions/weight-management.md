## Who should see an internist about this

- Your weight keeps rising despite your efforts, or returns after every diet.
- You want to know whether a thyroid, medicine or hormone problem is involved.
- Weight is affecting your sugar, blood pressure, knees, sleep or breathing.
- You have fatty liver or prediabetes and have been told weight loss is the main treatment.
- You are thinking about weight-loss medicines and want an honest medical view.

## What happens at your visit

Dr. James takes a careful history: when the weight changed, your eating pattern, sleep, activity, stress, medicines and previous attempts. He measures weight, waist and blood pressure, and may check your thyroid, sugar and liver tests, because weight is often a symptom as much as a cause.

You then agree a realistic target and the first changes to make. Dr. James decides the right tests and treatment for you after examining you, so any medicine is chosen for your situation, not by trend.

## How to prepare

- Bring your medicine list and any older blood reports.
- If you can, keep a simple food and sleep diary for a few days before the visit.
- Bring older weight records or photos of reports from other clinics.

## After your visit

You leave with a target and a short list of first changes, not a long forbidden-food list. Follow-up visits check weight, waist and the related numbers like sugar and liver tests. The plan is adjusted when life or results change, so it keeps working.
