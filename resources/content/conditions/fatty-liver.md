## Who should see a physician about this

- An ultrasound report mentioned fatty liver or increased liver echogenicity.
- Liver enzymes (ALT, AST, GGT) came back raised on a blood test.
- You have diabetes, prediabetes, high cholesterol or central weight gain.
- You want to know whether your liver needs monitoring or specialist review.

## What happens at your visit

Dr. James asks about alcohol honestly, medicines and supplements, weight history, and your diabetes and cholesterol picture. He examines your abdomen and reviews your liver tests and any ultrasound report.

The job at this visit is twofold: confirm that fat is the likely explanation and rule out other causes like viral hepatitis, and estimate how much the liver is affected. Most people can then be managed with a plan targeting the drivers.

## How to prepare

- Bring all liver blood reports and ultrasound or scan reports.
- Bring your medicine and supplement list, including herbal products.
- Be ready to discuss alcohol intake honestly; it changes the interpretation completely.

## After your visit

You leave knowing what the liver finding means for you, which other causes have been excluded, and the plan: weight and sugar targets, a recheck date for liver tests, and whether a scan or specialist opinion is needed. Progress is measured on repeat tests, not guessed.
