## Who should see a physician about this

- A parent, brother or sister had a heart attack or stroke, especially before 60.
- You have diabetes, high blood pressure, high cholesterol, or you smoke.
- You are over 40 and have never had your heart risk measured.
- You want to start a fitness programme after a long inactive period.
- You get chest discomfort, breathlessness or palpitations on effort and want it checked.

## What happens at your visit

Dr. James takes a full history: family heart events, smoking, diet, activity, stress and any symptoms like chest tightness, breathlessness or palpitations. He measures your blood pressure, weight and waist, listens to your heart, and reviews or orders the relevant blood tests and an ECG if indicated.

Your results are then combined into a plain answer: low, medium or high risk, and which factors are doing most of the damage. You discuss which changes give you the largest benefit first.

## How to prepare

- Bring older reports of cholesterol, sugar and blood pressure.
- Bring your medicine and supplement list.
- Ask when booking whether fasting is needed, in case a fresh cholesterol or sugar test is taken.

## After your visit

You leave with your risk level explained and a short, prioritised plan: which numbers to improve first and how. Recheck dates are set so progress is measured, not assumed. If a finding needs a heart specialist's opinion, that is discussed openly and referred appropriately.

## When to get help straight away

Chest pain is an emergency when it is heavy, crushing or spreads to the arm, jaw or back, especially with sweating, sickness or breathlessness. Go to the nearest emergency department immediately. Do not wait to see if it passes, and do not drive yourself.
