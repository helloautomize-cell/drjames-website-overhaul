## Who should see an internist about this

- A blood test showed high cholesterol or high triglycerides.
- A close family member had a heart attack or stroke, especially before age 60.
- You have diabetes, high blood pressure, or you smoke.
- You started a cholesterol medicine and want the dose, side effects or targets reviewed.
- You have never had your cholesterol checked and want a baseline.

## What happens at your visit

Dr. James goes through your lipid profile (the fat breakdown in your blood test) and asks about diet, activity, smoking, alcohol and family history. He checks your blood pressure, weight and waist, because these change what your cholesterol numbers mean for you.

The key question is your overall heart risk, not one number. Two people with the same cholesterol can need very different treatment. Dr. James explains your risk plainly and agrees whether lifestyle change alone is safe, or whether a medicine would protect you better.

## How to prepare

- Bring your last lipid profile and any older reports for comparison.
- Bring a list of medicines and supplements you take.
- If a new blood test may be done, ask when you book whether fasting is needed, as some tests prefer a fasting sample.

## After your visit

You leave knowing your numbers and your target. If lifestyle change is the plan, you get clear goals for food, weight and activity, and a recheck date to see if it is working. If a medicine is needed, the reason and the follow-up schedule are explained before you start.
