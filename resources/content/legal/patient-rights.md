Good care is a partnership. This page sets out what you can expect from us when you consult Dr. James at Badr Al Samaa Royal Hospital, and what helps us care for you well.

## Your rights

**Respectful care.** You will be treated with courtesy and dignity, whatever your age, nationality, language or condition.

**Clear information.** You can expect a plain explanation of your condition, the options, what tests or treatments are for, and what they may cost, in a language you understand [CONFIRM: languages offered].

**Privacy and confidentiality.** Your consultations and records are confidential. Information is shared only with your consent, with those directly involved in your care, or where the law requires.

**Informed consent.** Tests and treatments are explained before they happen, and you may say no or ask for time to decide.

**Access to your records.** You may request copies of your medical records and reports, in line with hospital policy and Omani law.

**A second opinion.** You are free to seek another medical opinion at any time. It is your right, not an offence to us.

**To raise concerns.** You can raise a concern about your care without it affecting how you are treated. Concerns can be raised with the practice or through the hospital's patient relations process [CONFIRM: hospital complaint channel].

## Your responsibilities

- Give a full and honest history: symptoms, medicines, supplements, allergies, past illnesses and reports.
- Ask when something is not clear. There are no embarrassing questions in a consultation.
- Follow the agreed plan, or tell us honestly if you cannot or choose not to, so the plan can be adjusted.
- Keep appointments or cancel in reasonable time so the slot can go to another patient.
- Treat staff and other patients with the same courtesy you expect.

## Emergencies

In a medical emergency, go to the nearest emergency department. Do not wait for a website or WhatsApp reply.
