## Booking a visit

You can request an appointment in three ways: the form on the contact page, a WhatsApp message, or a call to +968 9751 0975.

Submitting a request is not a confirmed booking. The practice replies to confirm the exact time, usually within [CONFIRM: reply-time promise]. Requests outside consultation hours are answered when the clinic next opens.

## Consultation hours

Saturday to Thursday, 8:30 am to 9 pm. The clinic is closed on Fridays. Public holiday timings may differ [CONFIRM: holiday rule].

## Before your visit

- Bring your medicines or a clear photo of the boxes.
- Bring older reports, scans and discharge summaries.
- If blood tests may be needed, ask when booking whether you should come fasting.
- Bring your insurance card if you plan to use insurance [CONFIRM: insurance arrangements].

## Arriving

Please arrive a few minutes before your appointment, especially for a first visit, so registration does not eat into consultation time. If you are running late, message or call us so we can advise whether to come in or rebook.

## Cancelling or moving a visit

Life happens. If you cannot make it, tell us as early as you can by WhatsApp or phone and we will move the booking. Repeated no-shows without notice may mean future requests are asked to confirm on the day.

## Follow-up visits

Follow-up timing is set at your consultation based on what your condition needs, not on a fixed schedule. Reports and test results are explained at a review visit or, when suitable, by message.

## Emergencies

Appointments are for non-emergency care. In an emergency, go to the nearest emergency department rather than waiting for a booking reply.
