The content on this website is general health information written for education. It is not medical advice, a diagnosis or a prescription, and it must not be used to start, stop or change any medicine or treatment.

## Not a substitute for a consultation

Every patient is different. Information that is correct in general may not apply to your situation, your medicines, your other conditions or your body. The only safe way to get advice for your health is a consultation with a qualified physician who can examine you.

Reading this site does not create a doctor-patient relationship with Dr. James Kumar Pallivathukkal.

## Emergencies

Never use this website in an emergency and never delay emergency care because of anything read here. In a medical emergency in Oman, call 9999 or go to the nearest emergency department.

## Accuracy and review

Medical content on this site is drafted for education and reviewed by Dr. James before publication, as described in our editorial and medical review policy. Medicine changes over time, and while we review pages regularly, we cannot guarantee every page reflects the latest guidance at the moment you read it. Each medical page shows its last-reviewed date.

## No outcome promises

No page on this site promises a result, a fix or a timeline for recovery. Statements about what usually happens describe typical courses, not guarantees.

## Your responsibilities

Always tell your physician about all medicines, supplements and remedies you take, including herbal products, and follow the plan agreed at your own consultation rather than general information found online.
