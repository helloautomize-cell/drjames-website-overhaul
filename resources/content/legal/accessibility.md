We want this website usable by every patient, including people using screen readers, keyboards only, zoom, reduced motion settings, or small screens.

## What we build to

The site is developed and tested against WCAG 2.2 level AA. Practical measures include:

- Text and contrast large enough to read comfortably, with body text at 17 px or above.
- Full keyboard access, a visible focus ring, and a skip link on every page.
- Semantic landmarks, one clear heading structure per page, and labels on form fields.
- Support for reduced-motion settings: carousels and the hero cube stop moving when you prefer it.
- Plain-language content written to be understandable, not just compliant.

## Known limits

The site is new and we are still improving it. Third-party embeds such as Google Maps are outside our control, though they load only on request. If a PDF or scanned document is ever added, we will aim for an accessible alternative.
