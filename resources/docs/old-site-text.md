<h2> 2. Understanding Sleep Disorders and Their Impact on Health 
Sleep is essential for physical and mental well-being. Poor sleep can affect concentration, mood, immunity, and overall health. Sleep disorders are becoming increasingly common due to stress, lifestyle changes, and medical conditions. 
<h3> Common Sleep Disorders 
Insomnia 
Sleep apnea 
Restless leg syndrome 
Narcolepsy 
<h3> Symptoms of Sleep Problems 
Difficulty falling asleep 
Frequent nighttime waking 
Daytime fatigue 
Snoring and breathing interruptions 
Morning headaches 
<h3> Health Risks of Poor Sleep 
Chronic sleep problems can increase the risk of heart disease, diabetes, anxiety, depression, and weakened immunity. 
<h3> Ways to Improve Sleep Quality 
Seek medical advice for persistent sleep issues 
Follow a regular sleep schedule 
Avoid caffeine before bedtime 
Reduce screen time at night 
Create a comfortable sleep environment

<h1> Common Digestive Problems and How to Maintain Gut Health 
[IMG https://drjamesp.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/118-1.webp alt='Common Digestive Problems and How to Maintain Gut Health']
August 20, 2019 Uncategorized 0 
Digestive health plays an important role in overall wellness. Unhealthy eating habits, stress, and infections can lead to digestive problems that affect daily life. 
<h3> Common Digestive Disorders 
Acid reflux 
Gastritis 
Constipation 
Irritable bowel syndrome (IBS) 
Bloating and indigestion 
<h3> Symptoms to Watch For 
Abdominal pain 
Heartburn 
Nausea 
Frequent bloating 
Changes in bowel habits 
<h3> Tips for Better Digestive Health 
Eat balanced meals 
Drink plenty of water 
Avoid excessive oily and spicy foods 
Include fiber-rich foods in your diet 
Exercise regularly 
<h3> When to Consult a Specialist 
Persistent stomach pain, unexplained weight loss, blood in stool, or ongoing digestive discomfort should not be ignored and may require medical evaluation.
