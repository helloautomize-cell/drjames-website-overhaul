## 3. Work completed

- **Fixed all page titles.** A shared template appended the full doctor name to every title, pushing them to 63–95 characters (Google shows ~60). All now render at roughly half that length.
- **Rewrote 56 meta descriptions** (the grey text under each result in Google) that were too short or too long, across guides, condition pages, policy pages and the home page.
- **Rebuilt llms.txt from real site data.** It had the wrong clinic hours ("8:30 am–9 pm" instead of 9–1 and 5–9) and listed only 7 pages; it now lists all 63 guides, 18 conditions and every policy page, and updates itself automatically.
- **Made the sitemap honest.** Google was being told most pages changed at build time; each URL now reports its real last-edited date.
- **Gave all 63 guides their own social/AI share card** on Twitter/X (previously all shared one generic image) and fixed breadcrumb trails so the article itself appears.
- **Strengthened the topic clusters.** 9 older or thin guides now link back to their main guide and to 2 sibling guides each — nothing on the site is a dead end.
- **Removed a duplicate thyroid page** that competed with the main thyroid guide for the same Google query; its address permanently redirects to the full guide.
- **Made the review claim honest.** The 18 condition pages showed "Reviewed by Dr. James" with no review on file — the claim now appears only once a review date is recorded.
- **Linked your name to your profile page** from every article byline and review box (previously plain text — a wasted trust signal).
- **Expanded 33 very short FAQ answers** toward the 40–60 word sweet spot AI answers prefer.
- **Named all 11 AI crawlers** (Google, ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Apple, Bing) explicitly in robots.txt.
- **Cleaned the code:** removed an embedded "generated by AI" provenance receipt inside the site's icon files, removed leftover draft comments that appeared in page source, and normalised US spellings (anaemia, diarrhoea, fibre…) to match the rest of the site.
- **Added a web app manifest** and switched footer navigation to proper site links.
- **Verified everything on a fresh production build** — no errors, all checks pass.
