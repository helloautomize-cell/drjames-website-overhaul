## 1. Positioning and copy rules (all pages)
- Core identity: Specialist Internist. Never "Diabetes Specialist" as the identity, never "treats everything", never "longevity doctor".
- Promise: "A physician who sees the whole picture."
- Line under it: "Comprehensive internal medicine for long-term health."
- Four pillars, always in this order and wording: Diabetes and metabolic health · Complex adult medicine · Preventive care · Critical care experience.
  "Critical care experience" is a credential (he led ICU and COVID-19 operations), not a bookable service. Never link it to a booking reason.
- Specialties he does not hold (cardiology, neurology, rheumatology, haematology and so on) appear only as conditions he manages.
- No testimonials, ratings, patient numbers, "best", "leading", cure or guarantee claims, and no comparison with other doctors.
- Emergency line 9999 wherever symptoms are discussed. No em dashes or en dashes anywhere in copy.
