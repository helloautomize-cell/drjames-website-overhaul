# Pre-Launch Website Check For Dr. James

26 October 2026 - repository and `https://drjames.vercel.app/` only

## What This Means

The new Indian NMC advertising guidance favours factual information about a doctor's qualifications, services, location and appointments, rather than personal promotion or promises about results. It also addresses synthetic medical imagery and patient privacy.

Your practice is in Muscat, Oman. We need to confirm whether you retain Indian medical registration and which professional rules apply before calling any issue an Indian legal violation. Indian data-protection requirements also have a phased start date; we have not treated every future requirement as already enforceable.

## What We Found

The preview needs changes before launch against the requested NMC standard: **13 findings - 5 High and 8 Medium; no Critical findings.** Three are technical risks in existing code whose activating settings have not been established, rather than observed data leaks.

The main concern is that several images are documented as AI-generated but depict you and patient consultations as if photographed in real clinical settings. Other priorities are the explicit agency marketing credit, missing registration/licensing disclosure, promotional portrait placements and wording that promises complete diagnosis or error-free testing.

Some short treatment explanations need a balanced account of risks and variable response. The privacy policy leaves the site's operator blank. Condition pages also publish without recorded approval while still claiming medical review in search-engine data. The record/certificate presentation and repeated booking pitches inside health articles need review.

## Proposed Changes

- Remove synthetic doctor/patient scenes; an AI label alone is not enough. Keep genuine identification imagery only in low-risk contexts.
- Remove marketing credits and review the actual agency arrangement; do not simply hide prohibited work.
- Publish verified registration/licensing information, qualify absolute claims and balance existing treatment explanations.
- Complete the privacy notice, enforce genuine medical sign-off and keep education separate from repeated personal sales pitches.
- Remove the ambiguous record/certificate presentation unless substantiated; fix consent withdrawal, analytics minimisation and appointment-data logging before enabling affected features.

The appointment form already has an initially unticked consent box and a privacy link. Educational disclaimers are already present. These should be preserved.

Please provide your current Oman licence/status and any Indian council/SMR/NMR details, confirm the legal site operator and data-retention process, approve the imagery and wording changes, and confirm your review/sign-off records.
